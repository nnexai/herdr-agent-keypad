## Decisions Made

- `LedEvents` remains transient and contains no Codex usage or source copies; Codex and health truth continue to flow from the one committed `RenderState`.
- Semantic event identity and epoch are shared, but physical phase and zone selection remain grammar-owned so a synchronized press does not become a universal LED animation.
- SYNC is an immediate all-off fence at the central engine boundary, guaranteeing one full-mask hardware write through the existing deduplication/application path.
