## Trust boundaries

ASVS level: L1. The plan treats the current device, live bridge, human observations, and persisted evidence as untrusted until exact identity, freshness, schema, and checksum checks pass.

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Plan 07 deployment/logical artifacts → physical runner | Checksummed files become acceptance authority only after exact deployment-ID and binary equality. |
| Exact keypad and guarded bridge → device evidence | Hello, publication, focus results, metrics, and reconnect/reset identity changes cross into the evidence process. |
| Human observation → final acceptance | Explicit physical answers must remain separate from machine/framebuffer claims and cannot be synthesized. |
| Defect ledger → accepted status | A preserved failure may close only through same-row before/after evidence on a newly validated deployment. |
