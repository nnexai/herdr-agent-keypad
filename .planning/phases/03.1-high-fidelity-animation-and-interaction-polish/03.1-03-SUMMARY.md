## Decisions Made

- The budget lock cadence is authoritative: 33334 us for Signal Edge, Ledger, and Instrument, and 66668 us for Beacon. Beacon remains independently slower because its bounded field pass is the highest-cost renderer.
- The scheduler owns timing and degradation; theme files own only bounded primitive choreography. Ambient phase is presentation metadata and cannot alter snapshot identity, bindings, focus authority, or source truth.
- Static and fence frames are intentional complete presentations, not suppressed rendering. Empty tiles explicitly expose EMPTY and NO AGENT, and unavailable Codex data remains N/A/RESET N/A.
