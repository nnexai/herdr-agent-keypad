<threat_model>
## Trust boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Committed generation → presentation metadata | Presentation phases must not become a second authority for identity, freshness, or focus. |
| Scheduler → display flush | Decorative scheduling can consume CPU/SPI time but cannot delay input or urgent truth. |
| Staged generation → active binding | Sequential visual reveal crosses an authorization boundary until the final commit. |

## STRIDE threat register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-03.1-05 | Tampering | `AgentProjection::commitSnapshot` | high | mitigate | Disable bindings before reveal, discard interrupted transition state, compose complete staged frames, and activate only after final flush. |
| T-03.1-06 | Elevation of Privilege | `RenderState` presentation metadata | high | mitigate | Keep opaque identity/epoch/revision in committed bindings only; presentation fields cannot authorize `focus_intent`. |
| T-03.1-07 | Denial of Service | `AnimationScheduler` and Beacon/ambient callers | high | mitigate | Measured 30% limit, Full→Half→Static degradation, hardware-first polling, fixed storage, and skip decorative work first. |
| T-03.1-08 | Information Disclosure | Complete-frame render state | low | mitigate | Preserve bounded normalized copy only; no credentials, host paths, transcripts, or raw opaque IDs enter theme state. |
</threat_model>
