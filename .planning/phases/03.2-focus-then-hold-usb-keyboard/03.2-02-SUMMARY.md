## Decisions Made

- BoardAdapter is the only debounced physical lifecycle provider. Plan 03 may consume only its public masks/accessors and must not inspect raw board-library state.
- The earliest pending physical edge remains authoritative until that key is consumed. Later polls cannot move the timestamp forward, and consuming one key recomputes the remaining earliest edge.
- A release is a property of the current official sample only. Clearing `released_latch_` before each `board_.update()` prevents stale release truth from leaking into a later poll.
- Invalid readiness or physical-map state clears the provider and exposes zero masks/timestamps; no candidate, owner, release, or actionable gesture can be fabricated.
- Plan 00's restored frozen binary and validator PASS are prerequisite evidence only. The validator confirms decision lineage, not a new Plan 02 or product qualification.
