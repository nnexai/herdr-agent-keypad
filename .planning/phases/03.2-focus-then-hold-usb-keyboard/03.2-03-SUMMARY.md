## Decisions Made

- The active Space owner is physical and local. Focus, projection, LED, and presentation updates cannot transfer or prolong it.
- Every lifecycle event uses one controller path that clears authority first, attempts a fixed empty report, and requires a clean all-up frame before rearm.
- Safety failures (`release_all_failures`, `hid_not_ready`, `hid_send_failures`, and watchdog failures) are boot-monotonic; `resetCounters()` clears diagnostic transitions and maxima while preserving those failures.
- The metrics contract remains bounded and non-authoritative: counters and maxima observe producer transitions but never authorize HID or mutate controller state.
