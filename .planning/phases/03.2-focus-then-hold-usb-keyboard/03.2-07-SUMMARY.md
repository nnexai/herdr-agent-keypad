## Decisions Made

- Preserve each grammar's existing visual language and physical mapping rather than introducing a generic hold animation.
- Keep D-13 cancellation transient and LED-only; after the fixed pulse it returns directly to ordinary pending feedback.
- Carry physical-slot provenance on hold segments so central policy can fence hold cues without mistaking ordinary success or health colors for Space ownership.
- Keep the active owner on its physical slot across later focus changes and never allow another slot to inherit it.
