## Phase Goal

**As a** user of the delivered T-Keyboard S3 Pro, **I want to** operate it through a verified physical map and a host link selected from real measurements, **so that** later keypad behavior rests on the exact unit and a proven transport.

<objective>
Close CR-02, CR-03, CR-13 through CR-16 and the physical/recovery portions of WR-02/WR-03/WR-05 while preserving D-07 and D-09 through D-12.

Purpose: Turn reset/reconnect and physical confirmation into non-fabricable, action-specific, hash-bound observations on the exact paired unit.
Output: Fresh boot identity, strict D-12 recovery tour/resume, canonical physical evidence validation, and concrete recovery/physical proof locks.
</objective>
