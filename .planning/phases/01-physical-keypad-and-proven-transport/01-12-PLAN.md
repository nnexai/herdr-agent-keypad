Purpose: Replace the semantically false and checksum-mutated eligibility chain with complete measured proof while retaining it as rejected historical evidence.
Output: New immutable manifest/result/traffic graph, enforced latency/resource/interruption gates, and strict USB eligibility lock.
</objective>
