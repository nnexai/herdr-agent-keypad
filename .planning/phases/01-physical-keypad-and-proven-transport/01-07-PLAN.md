<objective>
On the D-08 evidence-eligible branch only, provision complete Wi-Fi station and mutual-TLS credentials through physical confirmation and qualify a single-adapter Wi-Fi candidate.

Purpose: Close the station-network credential, spoofing, rollback, and composition boundaries before final selection.
Output: Security-gated credential lifecycle, mutually authenticated Wi-Fi link, exact candidate composition, and immutable passing/failing Wi-Fi qualification.
</objective>
